How this treasury works

  • No owner, admin or deployer powers. Dev deployed it and has no more rights than a stranger.
  • Members change only by member vote (or leaving). The club never drops below 3 members.
  • One member, one vote. A proposal passes with more than half of the members it was created with.
  • Voting lasts 1 hour here. Approved decisions can be run by anyone, once, within 2 days; then they expire.
  • One spend can take at most 50% of the treasury and never more than it holds.
